     - High bets: higher chance for medium/high multipliers, jackpot still rare

     - ❌ Lose (×0)  
     - 🔁 Free Spin (extra spin without paying)  
     - 🎁 Mystery Prize (random multiplier ×2–×5)

2. **Wheel Animation**
   - The wheel should **spin instantly and generate a real-time GIF or MP4**.

   - The wheel is colorful and styled like PS99/UnbelievaBoat.
   - Each segment visually displays its multiplier.
   - Optional: pointer animation and sound effects.

3. **Discord Integration**
   - Sends the animation to the channel when a user spins.
   - After the spin, send an **embed or message** announcing:
     - Bet amount
     - Multiplier landed
     - Prize won
     - Updated gem balance
   - Buttons: Spin Again, Bet More, Claim Prize.
   - Support multiple users spinning independently.

4. **Logging**
   - Track each spin: user, bet, multiplier, prize won.
   - Optional: keep a local or database log for stats.

5. **Bonus Features**
   - Weighted probabilities should be configurable for small, medium, and high bets.
   - All features should work instantly and scale automatically with bet size.
